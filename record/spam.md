# The spam, with the counts

Jesse Nickles's posts about Chad Scira are not one argument made in one place. They are the same claims repeated until a search box or a model treats the repetition as agreement.

## His description of the scale

On [the harassment record](https://chad.so/jesse-nickles-harassment-and-defamation) he describes thousands of posts, across X, Quora, TripAdvisor, poliscirumors.com, EconJobRumors, and personal domains. That sentence is his account of the campaign. It is not a second count of the catalogs below. The catalogs are the posts someone else has already logged.

## Stack Exchange

[The Metasmoke catalog](https://chad.so/jesse-nickles-stack-exchange-spam-targeting-chad-scira) is 111 true-positive questions and answers, from 6 March 2026 through 31 July 2026, from 41 Stack Exchange accounts. The main site is Travel Stack Exchange. Academia and Japanese Stack Exchange appear as well.

The pattern in that catalog is disposable accounts. Several rename from a default user number into the accusation itself. The same text is then pasted onto unrelated answers, so the claim survives after a question is removed. Some of those posts were pushed into the Internet Archive and later cited as proof the post had existed. An archive of a removed spam post is still a spam post.

The catalog says many more questions, answers, and comments were never caught. The 111 are the caught set, not the whole set.

On 12 March 2026, accounts connected to Jesse Nickles received 100-year suspensions after a coordinated-abuse pattern. That moderation, and the later page about the moderator who enforced it, is [the Stack Exchange record](https://chad.so/jesse-nickles-harassment-and-defamation-of-rory-alsop-on-stack-exchange). The suspensions were not a penalty for one email. Rory Alsop is a victim of the spillover. Chad Scira is the person named in the posts those accounts were writing.

## TripAdvisor and Expat.com

[The removed-post archive](https://chad.so/false-defamatory-content) holds 201 pages. The first index date in that archive is 22 March 2026. The last is 14 June 2026. Every entry in it was verified removed from the source site on 14 June 2026. Quora and other forums are outside that archive. Removal is why the titles are preserved there, not because the titles became true on the way out.
