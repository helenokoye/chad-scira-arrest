# The companies he does not own

Chad Scira has published a direct statement. He is not a shareholder, director, or owner of Agents Co., Ltd., Thai Visa Centre, Thai Nexus, or the other companies named in the posts. He does not operate a visa business. The companies have their own owners. Pairing his name with a company is the post, not a share register.

The statement is on [the harassment record](https://chad.so/jesse-nickles-harassment-and-defamation) and on [the removed-post archive](https://chad.so/false-defamatory-content). A Thai Department of Business Development filing is the public check for who holds shares. A TripAdvisor title is not.

On 14 March 2026 the same pairing was aimed at THAI NEXUS POINT CO., LTD. Trustpilot and TripAdvisor posts presented that company as if it were his. It is not. He has no ownership, management, or beneficial interest in it. That incident is [recorded on its own page](https://chad.so/jesse-nickles-defames-chad-scira-and-thai-nexus).

The Thai case number that sits beside this campaign is not a conviction, and it is not a case about a share register. Agents Co., Ltd. filed a complaint on 13 August 2025. The report is Bang Kaeo Police Station, Daily Report Entry No. 4, Book 41/2568, Report No. 56, dated 13 August 2568, reference case 443/2567. The warrant carrying 443/2567 alleges defamation by advertising. Chad Scira's pages state that the complaint is the same conduct aimed at Thai companies, and that his archive is supporting material. Nobody in that file has been convicted by it.
