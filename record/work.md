# The work the spam leaves out

The posts describe a person who does not appear in the work record. The work record is public at [chad.so/experience](https://chad.so/experience), [chad.so/projects](https://chad.so/projects), [chad.so/oss-open-source-software](https://chad.so/oss-open-source-software), and [chad.so/enterprise-smb-software](https://chad.so/enterprise-smb-software). This page is a reading of those pages, not a second biography.

## Sony Pictures Imageworks Interactive

Web engineer, September 2007 to February 2010, Los Angeles. The campaigns named on the experience page include Spider-Man, Superbad, You Don't Mess with the Zohan, and Cloudy with a Chance of Meatballs, and early Twitter and Tumblr integrations on that work.

## TBWA Media Arts Lab, for Apple

Senior web engineer, September 2010 to April 2014, Los Angeles. The experience page says he led the move off Flash in Apple's advertising after Steve Jobs' order, on a team among the first to make that change. The tooling was a small HTML framework, about 5KB, and After Effects extensions that exported HTML5. That stack rendered iPhone and iTunes ads. The page puts the volume at more than 500 million impressions, including interactive sites and large takeovers on YouTube and Yahoo, where the rendering was audited so it could run inline with the publisher's own JavaScript.

## Two viral projects

Tumblr Cloud and Facebook Status Cloud, both from 2010. The projects page describes Tumblr Cloud as a word-cloud built from Tumblr data that reached millions of users, and Facebook Status Cloud as a real-time status cloud with rapid adoption.

## AuctionClub and Artory

AuctionClub took real-time feeds from hundreds of auction houses and normalized them into tens of millions of records. The company was acquired by Artory. The experience page lists a CTO role in Luxembourg beside that work.

At Artory he is listed in senior engineering from 2018 to 2025. He contributed data and analysis for The Art Market reports for 2019, 2020, 2021, and 2022, published with Art Basel and UBS. The pre-merger chief executive named on the page is Nanne Dekking. In 2025 Artory merged with Winston Art Group to form Winston Artory Group.

He has written that he relocated to Thailand in 2014 to work remotely for Artory. That date matters later, because a bank story told about him depends on pretending the move came after 2016.

## Since 2025

Founder and engineer, independent. The current work is enterprise and smaller-company systems that replace a vendor suite with a system built for one business: structured pipelines, tests, and rules that follow the company's own constraints. The experience page says the development is customer-funded, he keeps the intellectual property, and he is not raising.

## Open source, and other people's questions

He dates small open-source contributions from about 2010. The open-source page names task.js, template-colors, and node-requester among the React and Node.js work. The projects page says the answers he has written on Stack Overflow and similar forums have reached close to three million readers.
